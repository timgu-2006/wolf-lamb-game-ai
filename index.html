Set up lets you edit the position: click squares to cycle empty → lamb → wolf, drag pieces, then press Done. Click a piece then a square, or click a candidate badge to play it. Hover a candidate to see its line. ← → step through moves. Positive eval favours the Wolf side. Start node solver/server.js and the page switches to the solved tables: every value shown is the exact game result (W/D/L badges with the number of plies until the game ends with best play; \u2265 marks the rare cases where the 100-turn rule forces a longer win than the shortest line), ?? marks a move that changed the exact result, and the engine is only used to break ties.
Rules
  • Wolves move first. Any piece steps one square up, down, left or right into an empty square.
  • A wolf may also capture a lamb two squares away in a line if the square between is empty. The wolf jumps onto the lamb.
  • A side with no legal move on its turn loses. Wolves also win when only 3 lambs remain.
  • Threefold repetition or 100 turns without a capture is a draw.
